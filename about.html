About GMA Angus Ranch

A Legacy of Quality Registered Angus

For more than 35 years, GMA Angus Ranch has been dedicated to producing top-quality registered Black Angus cattle for commercial cattlemen.

Founded and operated by George M. Avila, the ranch is located at 7750 S Combs Road in Merced, California, in the heart of the San Joaquin Valley. Our program is built on a deep understanding of what commercial producers need: cattle that are fertile, structurally sound, productive, and profitable in real working conditions.

We focus on functional genetics — moderate-framed, easy-fleshing cattle with strong maternal traits, good feet and legs, and the ability to raise a good calf every year. Our selection decisions are driven by both phenotype and proven performance data, always with the commercial customer in mind.

Visitors are always welcome. We enjoy showing people our program and discussing how our genetics can work in their operation. Call ahead so we can give you our full attention.
