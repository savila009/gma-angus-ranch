About GMA Angus Ranch

Over 35 Years of Raising Quality Angus

GMA Angus Ranch has been producing registered Black Angus seedstock for commercial cattlemen for more than 35 years.

Located near Merced in California’s San Joaquin Valley, our program is built on practical genetics that perform in real-world conditions. We focus on fertility, structural soundness, calving ease, and the ability to raise a heavy calf — traits that matter to commercial producers.

Our cow herd is the foundation of everything we do. We select for females that breed back, stay in the herd, and raise quality calves year after year. Bulls offered for sale are developed with the commercial customer in mind.

We welcome visitors to the ranch. Call ahead and we’ll be glad to show you the cattle and discuss how our genetics can work in your operation.
