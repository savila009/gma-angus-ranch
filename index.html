Welcome to GMA Angus Ranch

At GMA Angus Ranch we have made every effort to produce top quality registered Angus cattle for the commercial cattleman for over 35 years.

Located in California’s San Joaquin Valley near Merced, our program focuses on functional, performance-oriented genetics that work in real-world commercial operations. We raise cattle that are structurally sound, fertile, and productive — the kind of animals that improve your bottom line.

Our herd is built on proven maternal strength, calving ease, and growth. Every animal we offer is selected with the commercial producer in mind.
