Breeding Philosophy

Functional Genetics for Commercial Success

Our breeding program is designed with one primary goal: to produce registered Angus seedstock that improve commercial herds.

We select for cattle that are structurally sound, fertile, and productive under real ranch conditions. We do not chase extremes or fads. Instead, we focus on balanced traits that matter to commercial producers year after year.

Key Traits We Emphasize

Fertility & Longevity — Cows that breed back and stay in the herd for many productive years.
Calving Ease — Moderate birth weights that allow heifers and cows to calve unassisted.
Maternal Merit — Strong mothering ability, good udders, and the capacity to raise a heavy calf.
Growth & Performance — Weaning and yearling weights that generate profit for commercial operations.
Structure & Soundness — Correct feet, legs, and overall structural integrity for longevity.
Docility — Cattle that are manageable and safe to work with.

We utilize artificial insemination and carefully selected natural service sires to introduce proven genetics while maintaining the maternal strength of our cow herd. Genetic testing is used to confirm parentage and enhance selection accuracy.

Every bull and female we offer is raised with the commercial customer in mind. We stand behind our cattle and are proud to help improve the herds of our customers.
